# MY LITERARY JOURNEY: A GREAT BOOKS DIGITAL PORTFOLIO

A modern, visually impressive, and fully functional digital portfolio representing growth as a reader, critical thinker, and student of literature.

---

## 🏛️ Website Sections

1. **Home (`/`)**
   - Monumental frontispiece masthead with monogram **AD** and title **My Literary Journey**.
   - Curator's Manifesto, reading statistics ticker, and direct gateways into all six sections.
   - Lead analysis preview and curated canonical pillars.

2. **Student Profile (`/profile`)**
   - Scholar identity for **Adrian Drake (A.D.)**, candidate for B.A. in Humanities & Literature.
   - Academic statement, intellectual trajectory, and the *Three Precepts of Reading* (Textual Primacy, Hermeneutic Charity, Existential Integration).
   - Six assessed critical competencies with mastery bars and seminar transcript breakdown.

3. **Literary Analyses (`/analyses`) & Reading Room (`/analyses/:id`)**
   - Five in-depth, research-grade analytical papers examining Plato, Dostoevsky, Mary Shelley, Shakespeare, and Albert Camus.
   - Reading Room features: real-time scroll progress bar, drop-cap opening, interactive **Marginalia Toggle**, and Chicago/MLA citations.

4. **My Favorite Masterpiece (`/masterpiece`)**
   - Crowning spotlight on **Dostoevsky's *The Brothers Karamazov*** (1880).
   - Critical defense of why this novel remains unrivaled.
   - Four pivotal scenes close-read (*Rebellion*, *The Grand Inquisitor*, *The Russian Monk*, *The Speech at the Stone*).
   - Seminar edition and translator details (Pevear & Volokhonsky).

5. **Summary of Learning (`/summary`)**
   - The five major intellectual paradigms mastered across the Great Books curriculum (from Socratic *aporia* to Machiavellian realism, theodicy, and interiority).
   - Mortimer Adler's Four Levels of Reading framework.
   - Cross-epoch comparative synthesis matrix across Classical Antiquity, Medieval & Renaissance, Enlightenment & Romantic, and Modern Existentialism.

6. **Personal Reflection (`/reflection`)**
   - Introspective essay on outgrowing intellectual vanity, learning "the miracle of attention", and finding anchors in Marcus Aurelius and Camus.
   - Interactive personal reflection inscriber (persisted in `localStorage`).

## 🧭 Navigation Bar

- **Monogram Logo:** Circular gold/crimson seal featuring **AD**.

- **Navigation Links to All Six Sections:**
  1. Home
  2. Student Profile
  3. Literary Analyses
  4. My Favorite Masterpiece
  5. Summary of Learning
  6. Personal Reflection
- **Desktop:** Horizontal navigation with generous spacing and an active navigation indicator (gold/crimson accent underline).
- **Mobile:** Functional hamburger button with a responsive slide-down panel and active badges.
- **Consistent Site-Wide:** Displayed persistently across all routes via [`Navbar.vue`](file:///c:/GreatBooks/src/components/Navbar.vue).
